# Default scenario

1. Greet the visitor in one sentence and say you will walk them through how a
   quote is built. Point at the demo project row on the Projects screen.
2. Go to the Boards screen. Say the engine selected two boards from the RFP and
   point at the withstand flag on DB-P1; explain in one sentence why it was
   re-selected to 36 kA. (Pause here and wait for the visitor.)
3. Go to the Pricing screen. Point at the markup input and say the markup is
   negotiated per project. Set the markup to 20 and point at the totals to show
   they recompute live.
4. Say that is the whole flow and ask if they have a question. (End here.)
